Privacy Policy

This page explains how information is collected, used, and protected when someone visits this portfolio website or contacts Fahad Ahmed Misu through it.

1. Information We Collect

When you use the contact form or WhatsApp link, the site may collect the information you choose to send, such as your name, email address, project details, and message.

Basic browser data such as device type, page views, and general usage patterns may also be collected by hosting or analytics services if enabled.

2. How Information Is Used

  • To reply to project inquiries and support requests.
  • To improve the website experience and content.
  • To communicate about services, if you request a follow-up.

3. Sharing Information

Personal information is not sold. Information is only shared when necessary to respond to your request, operate the website, or comply with legal obligations.

4. Cookies and Tracking

This website may use cookies or similar technologies for performance, security, or analytics. If any third-party tools are added later, their own privacy terms may also apply.

5. Contact

If you have questions about this policy, you can contact Fahad Ahmed Misu through the contact section on the homepage or by WhatsApp.
